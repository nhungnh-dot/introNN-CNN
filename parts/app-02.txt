    <div class="card">
      <div class="small muted">Section 2</div>
      <div class="h2">Before backpropagation: how do we measure error?</div>
      <p>In Section 1, the neuron produced an output \(y\). But during training, the network also needs to know whether that output is good or bad.</p>
      <p class="small">For each training example, we compare the prediction \(y\) with the desired value \(t\). We summarize that mismatch using a <strong>loss function</strong>.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card panel">
      <div class="h3">A simple loss function</div>
      <p class="small">For this lesson, we will use squared error:</p>
      <div class="card center math-cell">\(L=(y-t)^2\)</div>
      <p class="small">The loss is always nonnegative. It is zero only when the prediction exactly matches the target.</p>
