{new}}=w_{i,\mathrm{old}}-\alpha\frac{\partial L}{\partial w_i}\)</div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <p class="small">After many training examples and many updates, filters that help lower the loss are reinforced, while unhelpful filter weights are changed.</p>

      <div class="card panel small">
        <strong>Connection to Sections 2 and 3:</strong> nothing fundamentally new is happening mathematically. A CNN simply has many more weights and many more intermediate functions, so backpropagation applies the same chain-rule idea repeatedly through a larger computation.
      </div>
    </div>
